# Walk Through The Leaders Book

Start your day, meet your team, and keep important records close. Follow the tour to explore your digital Army leader’s book.

[Watch the Phone Walkthrough](Compact-Click-Through.gif) · [Watch the Side-by-Side Walkthrough](Expanded-Click-Through.gif) · [Download More Screenshots and Both Walkthroughs](Remaining-Screenshots-And-Walkthroughs.zip)

The phone view takes you through one screen at a time. The side-by-side view keeps your roster beside the Soldier’s profile.

## 1. Unlock Your Records

Start with your records locked. Tap Unlock Records to open your leader’s book. [Phone View](Compact/01-Compact-Locked-Front.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/01-Expanded-Locked-Front.png)

## 2. Start Your Day

Today brings your team count, training reminders, latest fitness result, and everyday tools into one place. [Phone View](Compact/02-Compact-Front-Cover-Today.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/02-Expanded-Front-Cover-Today.png)

## 3. Meet Your Team

Browse your roster by name and rank. Open a Soldier’s profile to see their record. [Phone View](Compact/03-Compact-Soldiers-Roster.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/03-Expanded-Soldiers-Roster.png)

## 4. Open a Soldier Profile

See a Soldier’s photo, rank, assignment, and training together. The profile also brings their saved fitness and height and weight records into view. [Phone View](Compact/04-Compact-Soldier-Profile.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/04-Expanded-Soldier-Profile.png)

## 5. Review Completed Training

Scan completed courses, completion dates, and certificate status. Alternating row colors help you follow each entry. [Phone View](Compact/05-Compact-Soldier-Training.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/05-Expanded-Soldier-Training.png)

## 6. Add a Training Record

Enter the course title, add completion dates, and attach a certificate. Keep each achievement with the Soldier’s record. [Phone View](Compact/06-Compact-Add-Training-Record.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/06-Expanded-Add-Training-Record.png)

## 7. Score an Army Fitness Test

Choose a standard and age band, then enter the five event results. Each event shows its score and a Go or No Go result. [Phone View](Compact/07-Compact-AFT-Calculator.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/07-Expanded-AFT-Calculator.png)

## 8. Use the HT/WT Calculator

Enter height and waist measurements in matching units to see the waist-to-height ratio. [Phone View](Compact/08-Compact-HT-WT-Calculator.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/08-Expanded-HT-WT-Calculator.png)

## 9. Follow Troop Leading Procedures

Work through the eight steps, from Receive the Mission to Supervise and Refine. Open a step for its summary and leader action. [Phone View](Compact/09-Compact-Troop-Leading-Procedures.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/09-Expanded-Troop-Leading-Procedures.png)

## 10. Explore Army Guides

Find field references for Army leadership, the NCO guide, and holistic health and fitness. Search for the topic you need. [Phone View](Compact/10-Compact-Army-Guides.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/10-Expanded-Army-Guides.png)

## 11. Find Army Resources

Reach the Army services you use, from pay and publications to fitness and email. [Phone View](Compact/11-Compact-Army-Resources.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/11-Expanded-Army-Resources.png)

## 12. Review Privacy and Data Controls

Learn how the app handles your records, backups, sharing, retention, and unsaved edits. [Phone View](Compact/12-Compact-Privacy-and-Data-Controls.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/12-Expanded-Privacy-and-Data-Controls.png)

## 13. Return to Your Soldier

Come back to the profile to review training, add a record, or update the Soldier’s information. [Phone View](Compact/13-Compact-Soldier-Profile-Return.png) · [Side-by-Side View](Expanded/13-Expanded-Soldier-Profile-Return.png)
